# Laptop Hardware Disassembly & Maintenance

## Project Overview

This project documents a hands-on laptop hardware maintenance exercise involving the complete disassembly and subsequent reassembly of a laptop.

The objective was to become more familiar with internal laptop components, understand the device's hardware layout, and gain practical experience handling and maintaining computer hardware.

The laptop was successfully reassembled and returned to functional operation.

## Disassembly & Component Identification

I completely disassembled the laptop to examine its internal hardware and understand how its components were installed and connected.

Work performed included:

- Removed the laptop's external casing and internal covers.
- Disassembled the internal hardware components.
- Examined the internal component layout and connections.
- Identified major hardware components and their placement.
- Gained familiarity with the laptop's internal construction and assembly.

## Hardware Maintenance

During the maintenance process, I:

- Accessed the laptop's cooling assembly.
- Removed the existing thermal paste.
- Applied fresh thermal paste.
- Reassembled the laptop's internal components.

## Reassembly & Functional Verification

After completing the disassembly and maintenance, I:

- Reinstalled the internal hardware components.
- Reconnected the internal connections.
- Reassembled the laptop casing.
- Verified that the laptop powered on and returned to functional operation.

## Technical Skills Demonstrated

- Laptop hardware disassembly

- Thermal paste replacement
- Cooling assembly access
