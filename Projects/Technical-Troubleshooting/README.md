## Troubleshooting Approach

My general approach to technical troubleshooting involves:

1. Identifying the symptoms of the problem.
2. Gathering relevant information about the affected device or system.
3. Investigating possible causes.
4. Testing potential solutions.
5. Verifying whether the issue has been resolved.
6. Confirming that the system or device is functioning as expected.
